BitaxeNini V1.0 - PCB FABRICATION AND ASSEMBLY NOTES
====================================================
Generated 2026-11-17 from BitaxeNini_V1.0.kicad_pcb (KiCad 10.0.7).
If anything in the Gerber job file or drawings conflicts with these notes, these notes take precedence.


1. PCB FABRICATION
------------------
Board size          65.1 x 112.1 mm (outline: BitaxeNini_V1.0-Edge_Cuts.gm1)
Layers              4
Thickness           1.6 mm finished, FR-4, high-Tg (Tg >= 170 C recommended - high-current, hot board)
Copper              2 oz (70 um) OUTER layers (F.Cu, B.Cu), 1 oz (35 um) INNER layers (In1.Cu, In2.Cu)
Layer order         L1 F.Cu (top) / L2 In1.Cu / L3 In2.Cu / L4 B.Cu (bottom)
Stackup             Fab standard 4-layer 1.6 mm stackup is fine; no impedance control required
Surface finish      ENIG (required - large exposed copper areas under the heatsink, see 3)
Solder mask         Both sides, green (or any colour), LPI
Silkscreen          Both sides, white
Min track / space   0.20 mm / 0.20 mm
Min drill           0.30 mm (vias 0.3 mm drill / 0.6 mm pad, some 0.5 mm pad)
Plated holes        936 (see BitaxeNini_V1.0-PTH.drl, includes 4 plated slots)
Non-plated holes    7 (see BitaxeNini_V1.0-NPTH.drl - 2 x 3.2 mm are heatsink mounting holes)
Drill files         Excellon, metric, absolute origin, separate PTH / NPTH; drill maps included (PDF)
Castellations / V-score / impedance: none


2. VIA-IN-PAD - FILLED AND CAPPED (REQUIRED)
--------------------------------------------
- U8-U11 (KF1950 ASICs, 51 vias each) and U2 (TPS546 thermal pad, 15 vias) have vias inside SMD pads:
  0.3 mm drill / 0.6 mm pad, epoxy filled, copper capped and planarised (IPC-4761 Type VII).
- Filling and capping ALL 0.3 mm vias on the board (POFV) is acceptable and preferred if it simplifies processing.


3. INTENTIONAL SOLDER MASK OPENINGS - DO NOT "FIX"
--------------------------------------------------
- FRONT (top) side: the solder mask is deliberately removed over the large copper pours under the
  flat heatsink area (approx. 60 x 44 mm centred on the ASICs), INCLUDING over via caps and the area
  opposite each ASIC. Bare copper there is intentional (ENIG finish). Do not add mask or tenting.
- BOTTOM side: mask opening 3.3 x 5.3 mm under U2 (exposed GND pad for a stick-on heatsink).
- Silkscreen that falls inside a mask opening may be clipped by the fab - this is acceptable.


4. OTHER NOTES FOR THE FAB
--------------------------
- J1 and J9 footprints overlap and share the same two holes on purpose (alternative power connectors).
  Only J1 is populated.
- The Gerber job file (.gbrjob) stackup matches these notes (ENIG, 70 um outer / 35 um inner copper).


5. ASSEMBLY (PCBA)
------------------
- Double-sided SMT. TOP: TPS546 (U2), power stage, input/output capacitors, U1, J5.
  BOTTOM: the four KF1950 ASICs (U8-U11), ESP32-S3 module (U4), support parts.
- Through-hole: J1 (terminal block), J7, J8 (4-pin headers).
- BOM: BitaxeNini_V1.0_BOM.csv (manufacturer and DigiKey part numbers).
  Placement: BitaxeNini_V1.0-all-pos.csv (mm, KiCad origin, both sides; DNP parts excluded).
- KF1950 ASICs (U8-U11) are customer supplied (consigned) - not available from distributors.
- Do Not Populate: R12, R38, R43, R46, C14, C50 (alternate / option positions) and all test points.
- Test points (0.9V1, CLK2, CTSI1, RST_OUT1, RXD_OUT1, TPx, CLK1, RST_DET1) are bare pads - no part.
- Fiducials: FID1-FID3.


6. HAND ASSEMBLY AFTER PCBA (NOT IN THE PCBA BOM)
------------------------------------------------
- J4: 0.91" 128x32 SSD1306 I2C OLED module on a 1x4 2.54 mm header (bottom side, top-left corner).
  The module sits at the top of the header pins; it must clear the ESP32 module.
- HS1 ridged heatsink (68 x 68 mm M30 hashboard cut-off) on the BOTTOM side, ridges on the ASICs,
  with thermal interface material on the ASIC tops.
- Flat finned heatsink (60 x 44 x 44 mm) on the TOP side over the ASIC area, on ELECTRICALLY INSULATING
  thermal tape (the exposed copper pours are at different potentials).
- 12 x 12 x 5 mm heatsink on U2's bottom-side pad, on insulating thermal tape, centred at the U2 heatsink
  outline on B.Fab (must not touch the ridged heatsink).
- 2 x M3 screws through the heatsink holes (non-plated).
